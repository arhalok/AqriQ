* **Hackathon**: Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2026
* **Problem ID**: **SIH26033 (Problem Statement 33)**

* **Official Title**: *"Technology solution to reduce intermediaries, improve farmers' earnings and make consumer prices more transparent."*

---

## 2. The Core Agricultural Tragedy & The 5 Middlemen We Eliminate

In traditional Indian agriculture (e.g. Tomato supply from Kolar to Bengaluru):
1. **Village Petty Trader / Moneylender**: 3–5% margin for immediate cash advance.
2. **APMC Mandi Commission Agent (*Arhtiya*)**: 6–10% statutory commission + 2–4% cartage/hamali.
3. **Secondary Regional Trader**: 10–15% markup moving produce between districts.
4. **Urban Wholesale Middleman**: 12–18% markup for auctioning to city vendors.
5. **Unrefrigerated Logistics Wastage**: 25–30% of harvested produce rots in open tempos before reaching consumers.

### Resulting Economic Disparity:
* **Consumer Pays**: **₹ 40.00 / kg**
* **Farmer Receives**: **₹ 11.00 / kg** (Only **27.5%** of the consumer rupee).

---

## 3. How FarmFlow Kisan Solves PS 33

FarmFlow coordinates fragmented agricultural supply directly with commercial B2B buyers (FreshMart, Hotels) and Consumer Housing Society Clusters (e.g., Whitefield Residency - 120 households) using a demand-driven model:

$$\text{Demand Certainty} \rightarrow \text{FPO Pooling} \rightarrow \text{Direct Contract} \rightarrow \text{Spoke Weighing} \rightarrow \text{Reefer Line-Haul} \rightarrow \text{Instant Bank DBT}$$

### The FarmFlow Economic Transformation:
* **Consumer Pays**: **₹ 32.00 / kg** (**20% Cheaper** than retail supermarkets).
* **Farmer Receives**: **₹ 23.50 / kg** (**+113.6% Net Take-Home Earnings** directly in bank account).
* **Cold-Chain Transport & Spoke**: ₹ 5.50 / kg (Refrigerated line-haul + digital weighing).
* **Platform Fee**: ₹ 1.00 / kg.
* **Transit Spoilage**: Drops from **25–30% down to < 3%**.

## 4. Winning Features That Impress Hackathon Judges

1. **Farmer-First Clean Accessibility**:
   - High-contrast, large touch-friendly cards designed for Indian smallholders.
   - **5-Language Switcher**: English, हिन्दी (Hindi), ಕನ್ನಡ (Kannada), தமிழ் (Tamil), and తెలుగు (Telugu).
   - **"Kisan Vani" Voice Assistant**: Speaks out live market rates, weather warnings, and crop advisories in local audio.
2. **Live APMC Mandi vs FarmFlow Net Realization Comparator**:
   - Side-by-side transparent calculation showing every deduction (Arhtiya cut, Hamali, Weighment loss vs Direct Reefer transport).
   - Interactive quantity slider demonstrating exact thousands of extra rupees earned.
3. **AI Plant Doctor ("Kisan Doctor")**:
   - Visual leaf diagnostic scanner for Early Blight, Late Blight, and Leaf Curl Virus.
   - Verified organic remedies (Neem, Trichoderma) and ICAR chemical solutions.
4. **Hyperlocal Agro-Weather & Spray Advisor**:
   - Prevents crop damage by recommending optimal spraying windows based on humidity and rain probability.
5. **Solar Micro-Cold Storage Booking**:
   - Allows farmers to store crates at village farm-gate cold rooms to avoid distress selling during market gluts.
6. **Digital Weighbridge Slip & Instant Bank DBT Transfer**:
   - Tamper-proof QR code verification and simulated Aadhaar DBT payout receipt.
7. **Resilient Self-Healing Supply Chain**:
   - Live simulated **NH-75 truck breakdown recovery** with automated standby reefer dispatch.
   - Live simulated **150 kg spoke shortage mitigation** via dynamic solar cold reserve buffer.
   - Live simulated **buyer 7-day credit grace extension** protected by smart escrow lien.
8. **Zero Backend Required (Instant Browser Execution)**:
   - Double-clicking `index.html` runs seamlessly in modern browsers with zero setup, zero dependencies, and zero CORS errors.
